# DOOM (2016) and DOOM ETERNAL: Push-Forward Combat and Arena Design (lessons for a fast 3/4 top-down sci-fi action game)

## 1. The resource loop: glory kills, chainsaw, flame belch, blood punch, ammo scarcity, weak points ("combat chess")

### Takeaway
id took the resources other shooters hand out passively (regenerating health, ammo crates, cover) and put them inside enemies. Each resource comes from a different verb: glory kill gives health, chainsaw gives ammo, flame belch gives armor. Stock is kept deliberately small, so the player has to close distance and keep killing. Doom 2016 introduced this ("push-forward combat"). Eternal hardened it with lower ammo caps, fewer pickups, a fuel/cooldown economy and weak points ("speed chess").

### Cited Findings
**Philosophy (Doom 2016, GDC 2018 "Embracing Push Forward Combat in DOOM", Kurt Loudy and Jake Campbell)**
- The GDC Vault abstract says push-forward combat is used as "a filter we use to judge whether a mechanic is appropriate for the game". It goes against genre conventions like regenerating health, reloading and cover, and "puts the onus on the player to take what they need from adversaries". — [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1024940/Embracing-Push-Forward-Combat-in) (abstract via search snippet)
- The speakers listed four core pillars: fast movement, unique weapons, strong AI archetypes, and the player's brute strength. They said the Doom Marine "is a relentless force and having them stop to regen health, or disengage to go resource hunting, didn't jibe with 'push forward combat.'" — [GDC speaker Q&A](https://gdconf.com/news/speaker-qa-kurt-loudy-jake-campbell-break-combat-philosophy-doom) (search snippet; page returned 404 when fetched)
- Loudy's stated goal was combat where you "must keep moving or die" (動き続けなければ死ぬ). Health is recovered by killing, not hiding, and there is no reloading. — [GAME Watch GDC 2018 report](https://game.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1113403.html)
- The talk's intended loop: shotgun one target, rocket another, melee/finish weakened enemies, chainsaw when out of ammo. The chainsaw was framed as "a finite but powerful ammo refill", not a melee weapon. — [GAME Watch](https://game.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1113403.html)
- Doom 4 (2007–2011, cancelled) was influenced by Call of Duty: passive health regen, cover-based play, scripted cinematics, nicknamed "Call of Doom". The glory kill began as a "sync melee" system in Doom 4 and became central once rewarding aggression with resources was adopted. — [Wikipedia: Doom (2016)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_(2016_video_game))

**Glory kills (health)**
- Sustained damage triggers a ladder of pain reactions: mild twitch, then falter/push-back, then a stagger state that enables a glory kill. The player is invulnerable during the glory kill, larger demons drop more health, and chainsaw kills give ammo. Ammo drops from other sources are kept consistent. — [Game Developer: Cyber-demons, the AI of DOOM 2016](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/cyber-demons-the-ai-of-doom-2016-)
- When an enemy is weak enough, its body glows and it is stunned, signalling the finisher. Glory kills grant "a large, clearly signaled health pickup". — [GAME Watch](https://game.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1113403.html)
- id CTO Robert Duffy on glory kill length: "We have strict time limits on how long they can last… They're hundreds of milliseconds, just because you want to keep the player moving." The animations are context-sensitive to the player's position and angle. — [Bethesda: The Guts and Gore of DOOM Glory Kills](https://bethesda.net/en-AU/news/the-guts-and-gore-of-doom-glory-kills)
- Glory kills make enemies drop more ammo and health. Staggered enemies near walls can have their heads smashed into them. In a pre-release build, glory kills also granted a temporary, chainable speed boost. — [Bethesda: Inside DOOM's brutally fast single player campaign](https://bethesda.net/en-US/news/inside-doom's-brutally-fast-single-player-campaign)
- Eternal: glory kills only become available after an enemy is staggered first, and accumulated glory kills recharge the Blood Punch. — [Wikipedia: Doom Eternal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_Eternal)

**Chainsaw (ammo)**
- Doom 2016: chainsaw kills produce an ammo burst, but fuel "is in short supply" and bigger foes need more fuel. Marty Stratton on spending ammo freely: "If you only have 10 rockets left, you don't have to save them." — [Bethesda campaign preview](https://bethesda.net/en-US/news/inside-doom's-brutally-fast-single-player-campaign)
- Eternal: fuel maxes at 3 pips and the first pip recharges automatically over time — (search snippet summarising [Doom Fandom wiki: Chainsaw](https://doom.fandom.com/wiki/Chainsaw/Doom_Eternal) / TrueAchievements; page not fetched). Fodder and tentacles cost 1 pip, heavies cost 3. Super heavies, Lost Souls, Buff Totems and Cueballs cannot be chainsawed. — [TrueAchievements walkthrough](https://www.trueachievements.com/game/Doom-Eternal/walkthrough/2) (search snippet; page 403 when fetched)
- Community best practice is to chainsaw fodder rather than spend all 3 pips on a heavy, for example chaingun a Hell Knight, then saw a fodder demon to recover the ammo spent. — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/782330/discussions/0/3087760096562233539) (player opinion, search snippet)
- A guide reports that killed monsters drop more ammo when the player's supply is low, which acts as a dynamic safety net. — [Gamepressure: Doom Eternal Combat](https://www.gamepressure.com/doom-eternal/combat/z6d1a0)

**Flame belch (armor)**
- Flame belch sets demons on fire, and damaged burning demons drop armor pickups. — [Bethesda Slayers Club: Demon Slaying 101](https://slayersclub.bethesda.net/en-EU/article/doom-eternal-demon-slaying-101)
- It has a 25 s cooldown (12.5 s with the Quickdraw Belch suit upgrade). Burning demons shed armor shards over time, killing a burning demon drops more, and shotgun kills on burning enemies produce extra armor. — [DoomWiki: Flame belch](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Flame_belch) (search snippet; page 403 when fetched; single source)

**Blood punch**
- A powerful melee attack that recharges from glory kills and destroys enemy armor. — [Wikipedia: Doom Eternal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_Eternal); [Game Developer: Aggressive resource management](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-aggressive-resource-management-of-i-doom-eternal-i-)
- A guide describes it as a heavy melee that stuns and breaks shields, and the "Punch and Reave" rune makes blood-punch kills drop health. — [Gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/doom-eternal/combat/z6d1a0)

**Ammo scarcity and "aggressive resource management" (Eternal)**
- Each weapon holds less ammo than in Doom 2016 and arenas have fewer ammo pickups, so players must use the chainsaw more. BFG and Crucible ammo/charges can only be picked up in combat arenas. — [Wikipedia: Doom Eternal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_Eternal)
- Hugo Martin: "aggressively managing your resources is kind of a new feature in the game." Martin also relayed Stratton: "the guns are the tool and the AI is the problem." Martin prefers "speed chess" to "combat chess" because decisions are split-second. — [Game Developer: The aggressive resource management of DOOM Eternal](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-aggressive-resource-management-of-i-doom-eternal-i-)
- id wanted players to "take what [they] need from the enemies, not hide behind a wall and wait for it to magically happen". Martin called Doom 2016 "a one-trick pony". — [Wikipedia: Doom Eternal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_Eternal)
- Martin's summary of correct play: "Be aggressive, manage your resources, be mindful of the guns you use." The right way to play is the one he showed at QuakeCon 2018: switching weapons and mods, meathooking, flame belch for armor, glory kills instead of hunting med kits. — [Shacknews: Hell Razer, The Making of DOOM Eternal p.8](https://shacknews.com/article/116635/hell-razer-the-making-of-doom-eternal?page=8)
- "The solution to all the problems is to be aggressive." — Hugo Martin, [Intel Gaming Access](https://game.info.intel.com/gaming-access/doom-eternal-aggression-solves-every-problem) (search snippet)

**Weak points / "the right weapon for the right demon"**
- Doom 2016: each enemy has a clear counter weapon (for example shotgun for one type, rocket launcher for another). Fights are meant to feel like chess, with positioning and tactics learned over the campaign. — [GAME Watch](https://game.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1113403.html). Stratton described enemies as "chess pieces" that each counter different weapons. — [Bethesda campaign preview](https://bethesda.net/en-US/news/inside-doom's-brutally-fast-single-player-campaign)
- Eternal matchups work like rock-paper-scissors. Example: snipe a Mancubus's arm cannons with the Heavy Cannon's precision scope, then finish with the Super Shotgun. Martin: "We want combat puzzles that are worth your time to solve and master." — [Shacknews p.8](https://shacknews.com/article/116635/hell-razer-the-making-of-doom-eternal?page=8)
- Martin called Eternal a "fantasy combat puzzle" in which the player picks the best tool to exploit enemy weaknesses. The Destructible Demons system degrades bodies as they take damage and lets players strip away attacks. Critics later called the weak-point system restrictive. — [Wikipedia: Doom Eternal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_Eternal)
- Weak-point examples cited by an analyst: destroying the Arachnotron's turret stops its explosive barrage, and Cacodemons are weak to grenades (eating one sets up a glory kill). Lower ammo pools force right-weapon-for-the-threat choices. — [Game Developer: The Dark Souls Design of DOOM Eternal](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-dark-souls-design-of-doom-eternal)
- Carcass example: it spawns energy shields close to the player, which block glory kills and paths. A rocket there would hurt the player, so you shotgun the Carcass first and then rocket the rest. — [Game Developer: aggressive resource management](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-aggressive-resource-management-of-i-doom-eternal-i-)
- An analyst notes that once ammo is effectively renewable, each weapon needs a specific role, such as the ballista against flyers or shotgun grenades against weak points. — [Game Developer: Pushing "Push Forward" Combat With Gameplay](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/pushing-push-forward-combat-with-gameplay)

### Inferences
- Each resource is tied to a different verb and range: glory kill and chainsaw are melee range, flame belch is short-range AoE, and ammo comes from guns at range. Recovering resources therefore always means closing distance. This transfers directly to a top-down game: executions for health, a fuel-limited "harvest" for ammo or energy, and an AoE ignite for shields/armor. (Inference)
- The 1-pip vs 3-pip chainsaw cost is a small, legible economy. It makes fodder the resource pool and heavies the spending decision. Copy this pattern: cheap resupply from weak enemies, expensive or impossible resupply from strong ones, so fodder must always be present in every arena. (Inference from TrueAchievements data + analyst descriptions)
- Short, regenerating cooldowns (first chainsaw pip, flame belch 25 s) beat finite stocks for avoiding hoarding. This matches the "short-term limits over long-term limits" lesson ([Game Developer: hoarder syndrome](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-doom-s-push-forward-design-cured-my-hoarder-syndrome)). (Inference)
- Glory kills capped at "hundreds of milliseconds" with i-frames are the key constraint for an execution system in a fast top-down game. Executions must be very short, grant invulnerability, and ideally be cancellable or chainable so they do not stall momentum. (Inference)

### Gaps
- Exact Doom 2016 numbers (chainsaw fuel per enemy type, health per glory kill, ammo caps) could not be verified; doomwiki/fandom were blocked. From memory (unverified): Doom 2016 chainsaw fuel cost scaled roughly 1 (fodder) / 3 (mid) / 5 (Baron-class).
- Exact Eternal health per glory kill, ammo caps per weapon (versus 2016), blood punch charges, and first-pip regen time were not found in accessible sources.
- The GDC 2018 talk slides and video were not accessible. Iterations the speakers described (previz videos; early prototypes) are only summarised in GAME Watch.

## 2. Enemy roster design: fodder vs heavy vs super-heavy, roles, composition, simultaneous enemies, waves

### Takeaway
Doom builds encounters from three tiers. Fodder are resource dispensers and distractions. Heavies are the real threats, usually with a primary and secondary attack and a weak point. Super-heavies (and "rule-breakers" like the Marauder) restructure the fight. AI is deliberately tuned to pressure without being unfair: attack tokens cap simultaneous attackers, enemies take "exposed" positions, and accuracy drops as player speed rises. In Eternal, telegraphs were sped up to match the faster player.

### Cited Findings
- Doom 2016 has 16 AI archetypes, each with unique behaviours and animations. Ranged demons (imps, cacodemons, mancubus) keep distance, Pinkies charge, and Hell Knights lunge after environment checks. — [Game Developer: AI of DOOM 2016](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/cyber-demons-the-ai-of-doom-2016-)
- **Attack tokens:** each attack type (melee, ranged, charged, etc.) has a limited token pool. A demon must hold a token to attack and returns it afterwards. Token counts scale with difficulty, and better-positioned demons can steal tokens so front-line demons don't idle. No specific numbers were published. — [Game Developer: AI of DOOM 2016](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/cyber-demons-the-ai-of-doom-2016-)
- **"Exposed cover":** Rage's cover system was inverted, so demons take open positions near cover that give the player maximum visibility. **Speed-weighted accuracy:** accuracy is a weighted distribution that drops as the player moves faster, and higher difficulties compensate. Enemies won't fire at explosive barrels near the player. — [Game Developer: AI of DOOM 2016](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/cyber-demons-the-ai-of-doom-2016-)
- Enemies intentionally miss: shots are spread by a probability distribution so they look aimed but miss, keeping pressure while preserving agency. Movement is randomised so enemies don't all move the same direction. Difficulty is tuned through accuracy and attack strength. Infighting between demons creates openings. — [GAME Watch](https://game.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1113403.html)
- Weapon-specific hit reactions: imps stop moving when hit by the assault rifle, and zombies stagger quickly under shotguns. A planned minigun-induced perpetual falter on the Cacodemon was removed before launch. — [Game Developer: AI of DOOM 2016](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/cyber-demons-the-ai-of-doom-2016-)
- In Eternal, mobility (dash, meathook) made the player "a souped-up racecar" that nothing could touch. Early builds were "a very simple, child-like puzzle". To compensate, the AI was made more responsive at the cost of visual flair: fireball windups are about 2x faster and the Mancubus AoE blast is nearly instant. Martin: "It's important that things react when they should react, with the speed they need to react." and "We're not trying to make a cinematic, immersive game." — [Game Developer Q&A with Hugo Martin](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-evolving-the-combat-design-of-id-software-s-i-doom-eternal-i-)
- Eternal has three tiers. Fodder die easily and supply resources. Grunts (imps, soldiers) distract. Heavy demons have far more health and usually a primary and secondary attack. On higher difficulties, demons can kill the player as easily as the player kills fodder. Armor works "as a second health bar" for enemies. — [Game Developer: Dark Souls Design of DOOM Eternal](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-dark-souls-design-of-doom-eternal)
- Grunts distract from "the real threat—the heavy demons"; super-heavies "raise the stakes when resources [are] in short supply". — (search snippets summarising [Game Developer: Dark Souls Design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-dark-souls-design-of-doom-eternal) and related reviews)
- Martin: "It's not a hard and fast rule for us that every heavy, for example, has to have a piece to him that you could blow off to affect his attacks." — [PC Gamer: destructible demon system](https://www.pcgamer.com/hugo-martin-doom-eternals-destructible-demon-system-makes-such-an-impact-2016s-doom-is-now-hard-to-play/) (search snippet; article body not retrievable)
- Martin called the demons his "chess pieces" and said they may frustrate players "as long as we're teaching" (Noclip interview). — [Player.One: Doom Eternal director discusses new and classic demons](https://player.one/doom-eternal-director-discusses-new-and-classic-demons-132526) (search snippet; page not fetched)
- **Marauder:** Martin says its most basic lesson is weapon swapping, and that the game is about clearing an arena as efficiently as possible. It forces players to flip from aggressive play against fodder to restrained, reactive play. — [PC Gamer: the Marauder is good, actually](https://pcgamer.com/doom-eternals-director-says-the-marauder-is-good-actually) (search snippet; body not retrievable). An analyst describes it as a shield that counters every attack, so the player must stun it and then land heavy hits, which makes most other tools useless. — [Game Developer: Dark Souls Design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-dark-souls-design-of-doom-eternal)
- The Buff Totem's presence immediately sets a new primary goal (destroy it). — [Epic Games Store: Seven demons…](https://store.epicgames.com/news/seven-demons-that-reveal-the-infernal-depths-of-doom-eternal-s-combat) (search snippet; 403 on fetch)
- The Arachnotron AI attacks players who peek-a-boo instead of engaging, which pushes aggression. — [Game Developer: aggressive resource management](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-aggressive-resource-management-of-i-doom-eternal-i-)
- Analysts describe two ways to keep an overpowered player challenged. One is overwhelming them with simultaneous demands (health, resources, movement, target priority). The other is "rule-breakers" that disrupt the core loop (Marauder; Ultrakill's V2). Rule-breakers risk frustration if they negate core mechanics. — [Game Developer: Pushing Push Forward Combat](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/pushing-push-forward-combat-with-gameplay)
- Higher difficulties in Eternal make enemies attack more often and deal more damage, rather than changing behaviour as in 2016. — [Wikipedia: Doom Eternal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_Eternal)
- **Scale:** Wikipedia says id "added twice as many enemy types". Stratton said "We've actually doubled the demon count in Doom Eternal" — [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/66268/doom-eternal-double-demons/index.html) (search snippet). He also said the game is "about double the size of Doom 2016" — [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/doom-eternal-is-double-the-size-of-doom-2016/). Sources disagree on whether "double" refers to types or on-screen counts.
- **Waves/structure:** Doom 2016's Titan's Realm has "relentless enemy waves" that push faster movement and strategic glory kills. — [Bethesda campaign preview](https://bethesda.net/en-US/news/inside-doom's-brutally-fast-single-player-campaign). Eternal missions have a meter tracking remaining combat encounters. — [Wikipedia: Doom Eternal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_Eternal). Escalation runs from mobs of imps, zombies, Arachnotrons and Cacodemons. — [Shacknews p.8](https://shacknews.com/article/116635/hell-razer-the-making-of-doom-eternal?page=8)
- An analyst criticises Eternal for information overload and for enemy spawn tells that need to be more pronounced. — [Game Developer: Dark Souls Design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-dark-souls-design-of-doom-eternal)

### Inferences
- **Attack tokens and speed-weighted accuracy are the single most transferable AI technique** for a top-down horde game. They let you fill the screen with enemies while capping how many fire at once, and they reward movement directly by making a moving player harder to hit. (Inference)
- A practical composition template inferred from these sources: always seed fodder (resource pumps), add 1–3 heavies as the "problem" with a readable weak point or counter weapon, and occasionally add one super-heavy or rule-breaker that forces a mode switch. Priority targets (totems, shield-casters like the Carcass) can be used to redirect attention mid-fight. (Inference)
- Telegraphs must match player speed. id sped up windups 2x after giving the player dash. In a fast top-down game, attack telegraphs should be short and highly legible (ground decals and silhouettes) rather than long cinematic windups. (Inference)

### Gaps
- No official number for maximum simultaneously active enemies or token pool sizes was found. "Double the demons" is ambiguous.
- No developer description of the spawn/wave scripting (triggers, reinforcement rules, heavy spawn timing) was accessible. GMTK's "Anatomy of a DOOM Eternal Fight" breaks down one encounter's spawns but could not be fetched (Doomworld/Patreon blocked).
- Full official fodder/heavy/super-heavy rosters and per-demon weak points could not be verified (doomwiki blocked). Commonly reported but unverified here: Cacodemon (grenade in mouth), Mancubus (arm cannons), Arachnotron (turret), Revenant (shoulder rockets), Marauder (shield is vulnerable during green-eye flash before melee), Spirit (microwave beam), Pain Elemental, Doom Hunter (sled shield, plasma). Super-heavies include Tyrant/Cyberdemon, Doom Hunter, Marauder, Archvile, Baron.

## 3. Arena design: lockdowns, shapes, verticality, jump pads/monkey bars, pickups, flow, traversal vs combat spaces, pacing, secrets, level length

### Takeaway
Doom 2016 alternated locked "skate park" arenas (medium–large, asymmetrical, multi-tiered, multiplayer-like) with hallways. Critics and id itself later called this structure repetitive. Eternal kept arenas central but enlarged them as a "racetrack" for a faster player. It added combat in between arenas, traversal tools (jump pads, swing bars, portals, climb walls), traversal/puzzle "palate" sections, a three-act level shape, and optional harder Slayer Gates. Both games use 13 missions.

### Cited Findings
- Doom 2016's multi-tiered, open arenas are called "skate parks" internally. ResOps (mission 2) introduces the first arena encounter. Lazarus has an arena full of heavies (Mancubus, Revenant). — [Bethesda campaign preview](https://bethesda.net/en-US/news/inside-doom's-brutally-fast-single-player-campaign)
- Arenas are medium to large, asymmetrical and vertical, "closer to multiplayer shooters than traditional single-player levels". Id calls the resulting puzzle of which demon to kill first and how to reach it "combat chess". — [Game Developer: AI of DOOM 2016](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/cyber-demons-the-ai-of-doom-2016-)
- Doom 2016 levels were built to encourage movement during combat. There are 13 levels with multiple pathways, open areas, secrets, collectibles (Doomguy figurines, data files) and a hidden lever per level that opens a classic Doom level area. — [Wikipedia: Doom (2016)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_(2016_video_game))
- **Lazarus Labs case study (id: Hugo Martin, Marty Stratton, lead level designer Jerry Keehan).** The level has three closed combat arenas. The medical lab's main room and two hallways had poor combat flow, so Martin added portals linking the hallways' dead ends into a loop instead of rebuilding. The crate room's line-of-sight breaks and multiple entrances/exits give escape routes and close-range enemy surprises. Keehan: "You realise how fast you're going when you start to shrink the space." Stratton: "We design first and then we figure out how to have it make enough sense to not be ridiculous." Checkpoint placement was tuned over many late playthroughs. — [PlayStation Blog: Classic Levels Deconstructed, Lazarus Labs](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2017/05/12/classic-levels-deconstructed-the-beautiful-brutality-of-dooms-lazarus-labs)
- Criticism: most nuDoom combat happens in locked-off arenas, unlike the 1993 original where combat and exploration intertwined. — [Critical Points (guest review)](https://critpoints.net/2020/03/15/doom-2016-review-guest-post-by-durandal) (search snippet). Forum users call the door-shut, spawn-until-dead loop repetitive and argue free-form arenas make encounters feel samey. — [Doomworld: Arena fights](https://www.doomworld.com/forum/topic/88780-arena-fights/) (search snippet; opinion)
- **Eternal sizing:** levels were enlarged because tight spaces left the fast player "banging against the walls". Martin's target is a "racetrack" that is neither too big (feels slow) nor too small (frustrating). The hallway→arena structure of 2016 was considered repetitive. Setpieces are planned early in level outlines by Martin, mission design director Jerry Keehan and the level designer ("Walking along the spear of a giant mech... is far more interesting than walking down a hallway"), but spectacle is balanced with traditional spaces. — [Game Developer Q&A with Hugo Martin](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-evolving-the-combat-design-of-id-software-s-i-doom-eternal-i-)
- **Combat between arenas:** in Eternal, enemies appear throughout levels, not only in arenas. Martin: "The combat between arenas is awesome; it's as good as the arenas in my opinion, if not better." Levels follow a three-act shape: "See something cool in the beginning, something new in the middle, and something fantastic at the end." Levels start from a mission design document and a graybox that designers rearrange "like Lego blocks". — [Shacknews p.6](https://shacknews.com/article/116635/hell-razer-the-making-of-doom-eternal?page=6)
- **Traversal/puzzles:** each mission has three puzzle types (punchable sliding rune blocks, attack-activated activators, shootable switches/barrels). Hazards include slime that prevents jumping and fireball turrets in hallways. Each traversal mechanic (double jump, dash, monkey-bar poles, climbable walls) is introduced alone, then combined. Most platforming is skill-based and replayable (Martin compares it to Tony Hawk). Martin: "Levels should challenge me, and be just as engaging to solve, as combat." Secrets are punched through walls. — [Shacknews p.6](https://shacknews.com/article/116635/hell-razer-the-making-of-doom-eternal?page=6)
- Arena traversal features in Eternal include swing bars, portals and jump pads, plus double jump, wall climbing and dash. There are 13 missions with optional Secret Encounters and Slayer Gates. Collectibles include albums, toys and cheat codes; upgrades come from Runes, Sentinel Crystals and Praetor Suit points; the Fortress of Doom hub spends Sentinel Batteries. — [Wikipedia: Doom Eternal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_Eternal)
- **Slayer Gates:** a key opens a gate to an optional arena that is "a notch or two harder" with more monsters. Health, armor and ammo are not refilled afterwards. Each gate awards a key/artifact toward the Unmaykr. They are meant to push players to their limit and test every weapon and traversal mechanic. Jump pads give access to higher ground. — [Shacknews p.8](https://shacknews.com/article/116635/hell-razer-the-making-of-doom-eternal?page=8)
- Pacing: Martin compares progression to martial-arts belts (white belt to black belt by Act 3) and says "I will put in the time so long as you're giving me a new gun once every five minutes." — [Game Developer Q&A](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-evolving-the-combat-design-of-id-software-s-i-doom-eternal-i-). "The content goes down, so the challenge has to go up." — [Shacknews p.8](https://shacknews.com/article/116635/hell-razer-the-making-of-doom-eternal?page=8). Each level introduces at least one new ability. — [Game Developer: Dark Souls Design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-dark-souls-design-of-doom-eternal)
- Criticism: Eternal's platforming was called "imprecise and momentum-breaking", introducing "points of friction in an otherwise frictionless experience" (Josh West). Critics also found the game bloated with progression systems. — [Wikipedia: Doom Eternal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_Eternal). Movement-restricting sections and tentacles break flow, and an analyst judged Eternal's level design somewhat behind 2016's. — [Game Developer: Dark Souls Design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-dark-souls-design-of-doom-eternal)

### Inferences
- **Arena checklist inferred for a top-down game:** loops, not dead ends (the Lazarus portal fix); asymmetry; several entry/exit points and line-of-sight breakers; elevation tiers connected by fast movers (jump pads or bars); arena size tuned to the player's dash distance and the camera's visible radius (the "racetrack"). (Inference)
- Top-down verticality is less legible than in FPS. Translate Doom's tiers into readable height layers (ledges, bridges, catwalks with drop shadows, jump-pad arcs) and into "through" routes such as portals/teleporters, which work very well top-down. (Inference)
- Because resources come from enemies, map pickups can be sparse and strategic: emergency health and armor on the circuit, and big-ticket ammo (the BFG/Crucible analogue) only inside arenas to reward engaging. (Inference from Wikipedia + push-forward analysis)
- Doom's pacing is a cycle of arena (high intensity), light combat/traversal/puzzle (recovery), and secret/collectible detours. Avoid monotone hallway→lockdown chains by putting small fights between arenas, as Eternal did. (Inference)
- Optional harder arenas that do not refill resources (Slayer Gates) are a ready-made "elite challenge room" pattern for a looter. (Inference)

### Gaps
- No developer statements were found on exact arena dimensions, encounter counts per level, or average mission length in minutes.
- Specific pickup-placement rules (e.g., health near arena edges) were not found from id sources.
- Lockdown mechanics (barrier gates, arena-start triggers) are well known from play, but no developer source describing their design rules was accessible.

## 4. Movement design: speed, double jump, dash, why constant movement matters

### Takeaway
Movement is the primary defence. There is no cover or crouch, AI accuracy falls with player speed, and resources only exist near enemies, so standing still is punished from several directions at once. Doom 2016 added double jump and ledge-climb to close distance. Eternal added a two-charge omnidirectional dash, meathook, monkey bars and climbable walls, then sped up AI and enlarged spaces to keep the player threatened.

### Cited Findings
- Stratton: "If you stand still, you will die." Players are faster than almost anything in the world, and movement is the main defence. There is no crouching or cover, so breaking line of sight requires moving. Weapon switching, mod swapping and movement were all made faster. — [Bethesda campaign preview](https://bethesda.net/en-US/news/inside-doom's-brutally-fast-single-player-campaign)
- Doom 2016's design pillars include fast movement. Reloading was cut because it disrupted flow, and double jumping was added to close distances faster. — [Game Developer: AI of DOOM 2016](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/cyber-demons-the-ai-of-doom-2016-)
- Doom 2016 movement includes double-jumps and ledge climbing. — [Wikipedia: Doom (2016)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_(2016_video_game))
- AI accuracy drops as the player moves faster, so speed is directly rewarded. — [Game Developer: AI of DOOM 2016](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/cyber-demons-the-ai-of-doom-2016-)
- Eternal's dash moves the player in any direction on the ground or in the air, with two dashes in quick succession before a cooldown. Dash Refills in some levels reset the cooldown. — [Bethesda: Demon Slaying 101](https://slayersclub.bethesda.net/en-EU/article/doom-eternal-demon-slaying-101)
- In Eternal, double jump comes earlier than in 2016, plus dash, monkey-bar poles and climbable walls. Wall-climb initially "wasn't balanced well enough". — [Shacknews p.6](https://shacknews.com/article/116635/hell-razer-the-making-of-doom-eternal?page=6)
- With dash and meathook the player became "a souped-up racecar" that nothing could touch, so AI reaction speed was raised and levels enlarged. — [Game Developer Q&A](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-evolving-the-combat-design-of-id-software-s-i-doom-eternal-i-)
- An analyst notes dash fights become "a chaotic mix of dashing, chainsaws, and grenades", and pushing into groups is necessary for health and ammo. — [Game Developer: Dark Souls Design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-dark-souls-design-of-doom-eternal)
- The GDC 2018 speakers framed the fantasy as Bruce Lee films — [GAME Watch](https://game.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1113403.html); PC Gamer headline: "Bruce Lee with a shotgun on a skateboard" — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/au/doom-feels-like-bruce-lee-with-a-shotgun-on-a-skateboard-says-id) (headline only)

### Inferences
- Constant movement emerges from four systems working together: no cover, speed-weighted enemy accuracy, resources located in enemies, and fast enemy projectiles. Copy all four. Any one alone (e.g., just a fast player) produces kiting, not pushing. (Inference)
- A two-charge dash with a cooldown and in-arena refill pickups maps cleanly to top-down. Dash refills placed on the arena loop can steer flow lines. (Inference)
- id's sequence of speeding up the player, then speeding up enemy windups and enlarging space, is a reminder to tune enemy telegraph times and arena size after locking player speed and dash distance. (Inference)

### Gaps
- No official movement-speed figures (units/second) or dash distance/cooldown values were found.

## 5. Feedback: hit reactions, gore/destructible demons, sound, music intensity tied to combat

### Takeaway
Feedback is built for readability at speed. There are big, readable animations, a laddered pain/stagger system, a glow when an enemy is glory-killable, weapon-specific hit reactions, and (in Eternal) bodies that visibly degrade and lose weapon parts. Music was briefed as primarily dynamic combat music, but accessible sources do not document how the adaptive system works.

### Cited Findings
- Enemy animations are big and readable, and the hurt state is clearly shown. Glory-killable enemies glow and are stunned. — [GAME Watch](https://game.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1113403.html)
- Pain reactions escalate with sustained damage from twitch to falter/push-back to stagger. Imps stop moving when hit by the assault rifle and zombies stagger under shotguns. Focus Tracking (IK on head, chest and hips) keeps demons facing the player. — [Game Developer: AI of DOOM 2016](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/cyber-demons-the-ai-of-doom-2016-)
- In Eternal, glory-killable enemies flicker/glow (guides say blue/orange). — [Gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/doom-eternal/combat/z6d1a0); [Twinfinite tips](https://twinfinite.net/guides/doom-eternal-tips-tricks/4) (search snippet)
- The Destructible Demons system makes bodies deteriorate as they take damage and lets players strip away attacks. — [Wikipedia: Doom Eternal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_Eternal). Weak spots let players "pick apart demons piece by piece", giving frequent feedback. — [Game Developer: aggressive resource management](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-aggressive-resource-management-of-i-doom-eternal-i-)
- Martin said the destructible demon system "makes such an impact" that 2016's Doom is now "hard to play". — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/hugo-martin-doom-eternals-destructible-demon-system-makes-such-an-impact-2016s-doom-is-now-hard-to-play/) (headline only; body not retrievable)
- Glory kills are hundreds of milliseconds long and context-dependent. A Mancubus glory kill is explosive. — [Bethesda: Guts and Gore of Glory Kills](https://bethesda.net/en-AU/news/the-guts-and-gore-of-doom-glory-kills)
- **Music:** id initially told Mick Gordon to avoid guitars/metal. He built synth-based music, layering sub-bass sine waves with white noise so it was audible on common speakers, and after 6–9 months he persuaded id to allow guitars (nine-string guitar plus the original game's chainsaw sample in the menu riff). Hell's music is more atonal and dissonant than Mars's. — [Wikipedia: Doom (2016)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_(2016_video_game)). Gordon's GDC 2017 talk "DOOM: Behind the Music" covers composition, production and philosophy. — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/gdc-behind-the-music-of-doom-with-mick-gordon)
- Gordon describes the game as one that "forces you to keep moving, judge your surroundings, be strategic in weapon choice and position, manage your health, armor and ammunition resources". — [Inverse interview](https://www.inverse.com/article/24596-doom-composer-mick-gordon-interview) (search snippet)
- Eternal's score added a recorded "heavy metal choir" of metal vocalists. — [Wikipedia: Doom Eternal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doom_Eternal)

### Inferences
- For a top-down camera, where enemies are small on screen, Doom's readability rules matter even more: oversized pain reactions and knockback, a strong colour-coded "executable" state, and visible part-breaks that change enemy silhouettes and attacks. (Inference)
- Weapon-specific hit reactions (AR pins imps, shotgun staggers zombies) give each weapon a felt identity beyond DPS and support the "right weapon for the right demon" model. (Inference)
- Common practice in this genre, and widely reported by Doom players, is music that ramps up at arena lockdown and resolves when the last enemy dies. This works as a strong "arena clear" signal. Not verified from id sources (see Gaps). (Inference)

### Gaps
- Accessible sources did not explain how Doom's adaptive music system works (layer/stem switching, intensity thresholds, arena-start/end cues). Secondary coverage (search snippet; specific page not verified) reports Gordon was told to put almost all his effort into dynamic combat music. The GDC 2017 talk video is the primary source but was not transcribed here.
- No developer details on gore tech (damage-state counts, gib rules) or on sound design for hit confirmation were found.

## 6. What developers said (GDC talks, interviews) and what analysts identify as transferable

### Takeaway
id's message is consistent across both games. Every mechanic is filtered by "does it push the player forward?" Resources live inside enemies. AI is a set of readable "chess pieces" tuned to pressure without cheating. Mastery is the goal ("white belt to black belt"). Analysts add that the model needs fodder as a resource pump and short cooldowns instead of finite stockpiles. The main risks are overload, rule-breaker frustration and flow-breaking traversal.

### Cited Findings
- **GDC 2018, "Embracing Push Forward Combat in DOOM" (Kurt Loudy, systems designer; Jake Campbell, lead programmer):** the push-forward filter; pillars of fast movement, distinct demons, distinct weapons and a powerful player; previz videos made first to fix the concept; contrast with Rage, where enemies pushed the player backward. — [GAME Watch](https://game.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1113403.html); [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1024940/Embracing-Push-Forward-Combat-in)
- An analyst summarising the GDC talk says id removed anything that pulled the player out of fights or blocked engagement, as a reaction to regen-health hiding and ammo hoarding. — [Game Developer: Pushing Push Forward Combat](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/pushing-push-forward-combat-with-gameplay)
- Transferable lessons from an analyst: integrate recovery into the core loop instead of consumables; prefer short-term limits ("use X every 20 seconds") to finite stock; reduce resource-management mental load so the question becomes "What is my answer to whatever is coming at me right now?" — [Game Developer: How Doom's Push Forward Design Cured my Hoarder Syndrome](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-doom-s-push-forward-design-cured-my-hoarder-syndrome)
- Players never enter a fight under-supplied and aren't punished for spending in earlier fights. Designers should provide in-combat replenishment rather than pickups or pauses. Push-forward moves FPS toward action games. — [Game Developer: Pushing Push Forward Combat](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/pushing-push-forward-combat-with-gameplay)
- Stratton: "Death is an ok thing, it's how you learn." He played the roughly hour-long E3 demo 30 times. — [Game Developer: aggressive resource management](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-aggressive-resource-management-of-i-doom-eternal-i-)
- Martin: "A hero is only as strong as the adversary they have to overcome." On a path to success: "If the AI and weapons are balanced, and there is a right way to play it, there is a path to success." — [Shacknews p.8](https://shacknews.com/article/116635/hell-razer-the-making-of-doom-eternal?page=8)
- Martin calls the game "a power fantasy… Doesn't mean it has to be any less sophisticated," with difficulty matched to player skill and mastery reachable at any skill level. — [Game Developer Q&A](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-evolving-the-combat-design-of-id-software-s-i-doom-eternal-i-)
- Stratton on upgrades: there are no "micro steps", and "You'll never regret your choices" (choices are not permanent). — [Bethesda campaign preview](https://bethesda.net/en-US/news/inside-doom's-brutally-fast-single-player-campaign)
- An analyst calls Eternal "more of an action game than an FPS" and "the Dark Souls of FPS" with crowds, saying you "cannot turn off your brain". He criticises info overload, weak spawn/reload tells, flow-breaking restrictions and progression bloat, and judges Doom 2016 better for flow. — [Game Developer: Dark Souls Design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-dark-souls-design-of-doom-eternal)

### Inferences
**Transfer to a fast 3/4 top-down twin-stick / action-looter (all inferences):**
- **Resource triangle in enemies:** execution gives health (short, i-framed, glow-signalled); a fuel-pip "harvest" attack gives ammo/energy (1 pip fodder, 3 pips elite, bosses immune, first pip regenerates); an AoE burn gives armor/shield shards over time. Keep stock caps low so the player cycles through all three.
- **AI governance:** attack tokens per attack type plus accuracy that falls with player speed let you fill the screen while keeping damage fair and rewarding motion. Enemies take open positions instead of hiding.
- **Roster:** fodder as resource pumps in every encounter; heavies with one readable weak point and a counter weapon; rare rule-breakers that force a mode switch, used sparingly because they can frustrate.
- **Arenas:** looped, asymmetrical spaces with multiple exits and LOS breakers, sized to dash range and camera view; elevation via jump pads, bars and teleporters; premium resources only in arenas.
- **Pacing:** small fights between arenas, a three-act level shape (cool → new → fantastic), one new tool or ability per level, and optional harder "gate" arenas without refills for loot.
- **Looter caveat:** progression bloat and stat-gated difficulty were criticised in Eternal. Keep upgrades meaningful ("no micro steps") and respec-able.
- **Top-down caveat:** Eternal's precision platforming was its most criticised element. In top-down, keep traversal fluid and combat-adjacent rather than separate platforming puzzles.

### Gaps
- No transcript of the GDC 2018 talk was available. Details such as rejected prototypes, the exact wording of tenets and any numbers presented are unknown.
- The GDC 2017 talk on Doom AI/animation ("Bringing Hell to Life", Jake Campbell) and the Noclip documentaries were not accessed directly.
- No analysis was found that specifically adapts Doom's loop to a top-down twin-stick game. All top-down transfer points above are this researcher's inferences.
- The arena-flow analysis in GMTK's "Anatomy of a DOOM Eternal Fight" could not be retrieved.
